2. Placement Guidelines
  • 2.1. The candidates aiming at "Higher Education (M.S / M.Tech / MBA) or Business" should not register for Training & Placement activities.
  • 2.2. Students must adhere to a formal dress code for all placement related activities.
  • 2.3. Students are required to submit a photo copy of their transcripts to Training & Placement Cell (TPC) as soon as they receive from AES. It is the responsibility of the students to update their academic records accordingly.
  • 2.4. Student who applied for placement drive and failed to attend the same will be barred from participating in the three consecutive future placement drives.
  • 2.5. A student who applies and gets shortlisted is bound to go through the entire selection process unless rejected midway by the company. Any student who withdraws deliberately in the midst of a selection process will be disallowed to attend the three consecutive placement drives.
  • 2.6. Students involved in any indiscipline activities during their course of study or in wrong practices during placement assessments will be barred from campus placement drives.
  • 2.7. Students shall not decline the first confirmed offer letter after willingly participating in the specific company's placement drive. Furthermore, students engaged in an internship with the company have to honor and join the same company.
  • 2.8. Students with a confirmed offer letter can apply for the next placement drive if the new offer's CTC is more than 1.5 times their current offer's CTC.
  • 2.9. Student without the confirmed PPO through summer internships shall be eligible for further placement drives. Later if PPO is confirmed, student has an option to choose either a confirmed PPO or offer received from the subsequent placement drive.
  • 2.10. Students from Core Engineering Branches are eligible for one core and one software placement drives. Such students shall choose one of the above offers and inform their offer acceptance decision to TPO immediately.
  • 2.11. Student who is placed shall abide by the company hiring policy like service agreement, mandated internship, joining date, work location etc.
  • 2.12. Students should refrain from communicating directly with company officials either in person, via email or a phone call under any circumstances. Communication between the company and a student must happen via the Placement Office only. Any unruly behavior compromising the reputation of the Institute shall deem the student ineligible for future placements.
  • 2.13. Normally, students will receive offer letters directly. In such cases, it is mandatory to inform the placement office and submit a copy of the offer letter.
  • 2.14. Institute is not responsible for any deferment or withdrawal of an offer letter by the company.
  • 2.15. Any violation of the above guidelines will result in disciplinary action & penalty.
  • 2.16. The Placement Office reserves the right to override any of the above rules in special circumstances and use their discretion to act on matters not covered by the above guidelines/rules.
